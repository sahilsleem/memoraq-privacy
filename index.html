Memoraq Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Memoraq handles your data. Memoraq is an independent, offline-first, private business archive tool. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta.

What Memoraq processes

Memoraq processes user-selected exported chat files (such as WhatsApp ZIP exports). It extracts, normalizes, and indexes these chats strictly on your device.

Where processing occurs

All data processing and indexing occurs entirely and exclusively on your local device.

What Memoraq's servers receive

Nothing. Memoraq does not have a backend server. We do not receive, store, or transmit your chats, messages, business data, or search queries.

Accounts

Memoraq does not require, create, or manage user accounts.

Chat data and Local Storage

Imported chat content remains in app-controlled local storage on your device. It is never sent to the cloud, shared with third parties, or accessed by us, unless you explicitly export it yourself.

Rescue Packs

Rescue Packs are user-controlled copies of your business archive. When you generate a Rescue Pack, it is saved locally to a destination of your choice. You control where these packs are stored (e.g., local storage, external drives, or your own cloud storage provider). Memoraq does not control or automatically upload these exports.

Billing

Purchases are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing. Memoraq receives an anonymized purchase token confirming your Pro entitlement, but we do not receive, collect, or store your personal payment credentials or credit card information.

Analytics & Advertising

Memoraq contains no advertising SDKs. There is no analytics SDK collecting your chat content or tracking your usage.

Data deletion (Delete Everything)

Memoraq provides a "Delete Everything" action in its settings. Using this action will permanently delete all imported business history, extracted attachments, temporary files, and search indices from Memoraq's local app storage.

External copies

Memoraq only deletes data stored within its own app container. It cannot and will not attempt to delete copies of data you have already exported outside the app, such as previously saved Rescue Packs or the original WhatsApp export files. Those remain entirely under your control.
